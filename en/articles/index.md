## Submitting Articles

Wrote a field case, a tool-chain combo (Burp / Frida / automation) or a setup walkthrough for AppInfoScanner? Submissions are welcome:

- **Published elsewhere**: submit the link via the [article submission template](https://github.com/kelvinBen/AppInfoScanner-Site/issues/new?template=article-submission.md); accepted entries are listed above with attribution and the original link.
- **Original unpublished**: paste the full text in an issue, or PR directly to `articles/` in this repo.

> The author's original blog is still live at [blog.52zhuanke.cn](https://blog.52zhuanke.cn/); only mobile-security related posts are mirrored here.
